## 9️⃣ What is happening in week 9

Last week you summarised and plotted single variables. This week you will start
plotting data sets with more than one variable. This means you need to be able
determine which variable is the response and which is the explanatory. You will
find out what is meant by "tidy" data and how to perform a simple data tidying
task. Finally you will discover how to save your figures and place them in
documents. The figures you learn how to make in this workshop will be like those
you want on your poster. The materials are on the VLE in the "Week 9 Data
Analysis in R" folder (or available outside the VLE [Week 9 without
2FA](https://3mmarand.github.io/R4BABS/r4babs1/week-9/overview.html)).

There is:

- An overview of the week.

- Some independent study to
  [Prepare!](https://3mmarand.github.io/R4BABS/r4babs1/week-9/study_before_workshop.html)
  for the workshop which will allow you to revise data import and read about
  summarising and plotting data with several variables.

- [workshop](https://3mmarand.github.io/R4BABS/r4babs1/week-9/workshop.html)
  where you will learn to summarise and plot datasets with more than one
  variable, and how to write figures to files. You will also get more practice
  with working directories, paths, importing data, formatting figures and the
  pipe. There will be seals 🦭, pigeons 🕊️ (the dove was the taxonomically
  nearest emoji) and bones 🦴. You'll make a figure about myoglobin in seals
  which is similar to what you might want for your poster
